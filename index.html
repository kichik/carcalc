Year by year

How it works: the calculator steps through every month. Each month it adds up both strategies' car costs: purchases (net of trade-in, plus sales tax), loan payments, insurance, maintenance, fuel and registration. Whichever strategy spent less that month puts the difference aside. With "Invest unused money" on, that money earns the investment return; with it off, it sits in cash. Net worth = savings + what the car would fetch as a trade-in − what's left on the loan. A loan still open at trade-in time is paid off at the swap: the trade-in covers what it can and the rest is rolled into the new loan (or paid in cash if the next car is bought outright). A car's value drops by the first-year % in year one, then by the yearly % after that. Insurance = a liability amount that rises with inflation, plus collision & comprehensive at a % of the car's current value, so it drops as the car ages. Maintenance starts at the base amount and goes up by the per-year amount each year the car ages. Taxes on investment gains are not modeled. This is not financial advice.
